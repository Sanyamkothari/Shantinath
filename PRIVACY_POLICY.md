# Privacy Policy for Shantinath Agro Agency

**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026

**Shantinath Agro Agency** ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your personal information is collected, used, and safeguarded through our application (the "App").

---

### 1. Information We Collect

To provide seed, fertilizer, and agricultural product ordering services, we collect the following types of information:

- **Personal Identification Details:** Name, phone number, email address, business/shop name, and delivery address provided during registration or order placement.
- **Order & Transaction Records:** Selected products, quantities, order history, delivery memos, and payment references.
- **Device & Technical Data:** Device identifiers, push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and app performance logs (Firebase Crashlytics).

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for essential business and service operations:

- Processing, managing, and fulfilling product orders.
- Sending real-time order updates, delivery memos, and account notifications.
- Verifying dealer/retailer accounts and preventing unauthorized access.
- Maintaining app security, performance, and user interface improvements.

---

### 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We **do not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We use trusted service providers for core infrastructure:

- **Firebase / Google Cloud Platform:** User authentication, database storage (Firestore), push notifications, and analytics.

---

### 4. Data Security

We implement technical safeguards including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and strict role-based access rules to protect your personal data from unauthorized access or alteration.

---

### 5. Data Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of your account data. To request account deletion or data removal, please contact our support team.
