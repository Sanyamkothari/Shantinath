Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 5, 2026

Welcome to Shantinath Agro Agency ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your information is collected and used through our mobile and web application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

To provide seed, fertilizer, and agricultural product ordering services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification Details: Name, phone number, email address, shop/business name, and delivery address provided during registration or order placement.
  • Order & Transaction Records: Order items, quantity, transaction history, delivery memos, and payment references.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device identifiers, push notification tokens (Firebase Messaging), and crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics) to ensure app performance and reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • Processing, managing, and delivering seed and fertilizer orders.
  • Sending order updates, invoices, and delivery status push notifications.
  • Verifying dealer/retailer accounts and preventing unauthorized access.
  • Improving app stability, performance, and user experience.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share data with trusted cloud infrastructure providers necessary to operate the App:

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): Authentication, Firestore database storage, cloud functions, and push notifications.

4. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit and secure Firebase access controls, to safeguard your personal data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and personal data. To request account deletion or data removal, please contact us at the details below.
